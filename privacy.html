Privacy policy

Media Stick · last updated 23 November 2026

Who is responsible

Media Stick is open-source software that runs on a TV box in your home. When you sign in to Google from it, the Google app on the consent screen is a Google Cloud project created by the owner of that box. The authors of Media Stick do not run any server and receive no data from your box.

What data is accessed

With your permission, the box uses the YouTube Data API to read your YouTube account data: subscriptions, playlists, watch history and recommendations, and to perform actions you start yourself on the TV, such as liking a video or subscribing to a channel.

Where it is kept

The access and refresh tokens and any YouTube data are stored only on your Media Stick device. They are not sent to the authors or to any third party. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to show your YouTube content on your own TV. Media Stick's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove it

Sign out on the box (Accounts → YouTube → Sign out) to delete the tokens from it, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/hanzhad/media-stick.
